    Task allocation computed by D0 –

    Every drone solves the same small assignment once per second: each task gets at most one drone, and the total of u = priority × capability match × battery factor − distance cost (+ a bonus for keeping the current task) is as high as possible. A drone only counts teammates it has heard from recently. It acts on its own row. No consensus step: if two drones heard different things, they can briefly claim the same task. That shows up as a conflict.
